About Me

I am a development professional with 8+ years of experience driving education reform through technology, pedagogy, and public systems. I have led statewide initiatives with Eckovation, UNICEF, IISER Pune, and now as Program Manager at Mantra4Change, where I enable system leadership to scale Project-Based Learning (PBL) across 29,000+ schools in Bihar.

Earlier, I spearheaded the creation of e-LOTS (e-Library of Teachers and Students), a digital learning platform now widely used across Bihar.

My work blends program design, solution design, operations, and capacity building, empowering headmasters, teachers, and officials to sustain change from state to classroom.

With a Computer Science background and recognition as a Microsoft Innovative Educator, I bring a unique mix of technical expertise, program management, and system-strengthening experience to advance equity and quality in education.
